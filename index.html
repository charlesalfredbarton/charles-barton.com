How I lead

Four principles I lead by.

  1. 01

    Customer first, then work backwards

    Every outcome and every team serves a customer need. I start there and work backwards to the solution.

  2. 02

    Start with business outcomes

    I align the leadership team on one clear outcome, with metrics and a named owner for each.

  3. 03

    Empowered teams and coaching

    I give the team the full picture, then coach them to own the outcome. They decide the how, and often the what.

  4. 04

    Choosing what to build with AI is the strategy

    AI makes building cheap. The strategy is choosing which problems are worth solving, and proving it in results.
